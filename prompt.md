## Full prompt (copy everything below)

```
Create a 9:16 high-end multi-character editorial poster about {{THEME}}.

If any of the fields below are empty, decide them yourself without asking: pick the visual structure that best fits the theme, derive a restrained editorial color palette from its mood, and write a natural 1–4 word English title.

VISUAL STRUCTURE: {{oversized typography / layered bands / geometric cuts / circular composition / freeform collage / irregular portrait grid}}
COLOR PALETTE: {{palette}}
TITLE: "{{TITLE}}"

Include 4–7 clearly different characters with distinct ages, roles, personalities, poses and relationships, and build a believable story for why they belong in the same poster.

Use the chosen visual structure as the main graphic system. Mix large, medium and small figures using full-body, half-body, close-up, side-profile and partial crops; avoid equal-sized people and evenly spaced layouts. Integrate the people with the typography and graphic shapes through overlap, masking, negative space, cropping and foreground/background relationships.

Use the color palette with bold editorial typography. The main title is the TITLE above. All visible text must be in English; secondary text should be meaningful — credits-style lines, a tagline, place or time cues — never filler.

Keep the composition rich, bold, story-driven and highly designed, with strong thumbnail impact and meaningful information density.

Style: contemporary cultural poster, film ensemble poster, independent magazine, Korean editorial design, European graphic design, realistic editorial photography.

Avoid: generic group photos, repeated faces, same-age characters, rigid cards, simple text-image blocks, regular grids, meaningless filler text, corporate poster styling, 3D type, watermarks, numbering, sparkle symbols.

Output one finished poster only. Do not show analysis or alternatives.
```

## 中文说明
一张 9:16 高端多人物编辑海报：4–7 位年龄、身份、性格各异且有故事关联的人物；一种主图形系统；大中小人物与多种裁切；人物与字体、图形叠压融合；所有文字为英文。
